# Quick Start: your first one-click button

An animated walkthrough, under three minutes

24 lines, 334 words (about 134 seconds of narration at 150 words per minute).

## Introduction

1. What if your customers could close their own support tickets with a single click? No reply, no login, no back and forth. `intro-1`
2. That's Klick-Zen. In the next couple of minutes, we'll build a Close Ticket button and put it into a real Zendesk email. `intro-2`

## Step 1: Start a new button

1. Klick-Zen lives right inside Zendesk. Open it from the sidebar `open-1`
2. Under Available KlickButtons, click "Create new button". `open-2`
3. First, give it an internal name, so your team can find it later. We'll call this one Ticket Close. `open-3`

## Step 2: Label the button

1. Now for the words your customer will see. Just type right on the button. `label-1`
2. Close Ticket. Short, clear, and it says exactly what will happen. `label-2`

## Step 3: Make it yours

1. It already works, but let's make it look the part. A friendly green background, `style-1`
2. white text, bold lettering, and softer corners. `style-2`
3. Klick-Zen hosts the button image for you, so it looks exactly like this in every email client. `style-3`

## Step 4: Choose what it does

1. Next, tell the button what to do. Click Add button action, and choose Set Ticket Status. `action-1`
2. Set the new status to Solved. That's the whole workflow. `action-2`
3. Does your Zendesk require an assignee before a ticket can be solved? Add one here, and turn on If Blank, so existing assignments stay untouched. `action-3`

## Step 5: Save it

1. Click Create. Klick-Zen saves your button and generates a ready-to-paste snippet. `save-1`
2. Copy it with one click. `save-2`

## Step 6: Add it to an email

1. Now put it where your customers will see it. In Zendesk, open a trigger that emails the requester. `email-1`
2. Write your message, then paste the snippet right below it. `email-2`
3. Save the trigger, and you're live. `email-3`

## What your customer sees

1. Here's what your customer receives. A friendly message, and one obvious button. `result-1`
2. One click, and they land on a confirmation page. Nothing else to do. `result-2`
3. And back in Zendesk, the ticket is already solved. No reply to read, and no agent time spent. `result-3`

## Next steps

1. That's your first Klick-Zen button, built in minutes. `outro-1`
2. From here, you can offer a Still need help choice, make buttons single use, or follow up with a one-click satisfaction survey. `outro-2`
3. Find Klick-Zen on the Zendesk Marketplace, and let your customers close the loop with a single click. `outro-3`
