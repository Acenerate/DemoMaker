# How Klick-Zen Works

An animated walkthrough, about two minutes

19 lines, 329 words (about 132 seconds of narration at 150 words per minute).

## Introduction

1. What if your customers could update their support tickets with a single click, right from an email? `intro-1`
2. That's Klick-Zen. Let's look at what it is, and how it works. `intro-2`

## Step 1: What Klick-Zen is

1. Klick-Zen is an app for Zendesk that adds one-click action buttons to your emails, agent replies, and Help Center. `what-1`
2. Each button does something useful to the ticket. Confirm it's resolved, route it to the right team, or approve a request. `what-2`
3. No login, no reply, and no trip to your support portal. Just one click. `what-3`

## Step 2: Build a button

1. You build buttons right inside Zendesk. Choose the words, colors and style to match your brand. `build-1`
2. Then pick what it does when clicked: set the status, add a tag, post a comment, and more. One button can do several actions at once. `build-2`

## Step 3: Add it to your emails

1. When it's ready, click Create, and Klick-Zen gives you a small snippet to copy. `send-1`
2. Paste it into a trigger, automation or macro, or drop a button straight into a reply from the ticket editor. `send-2`
3. Zendesk fills in the ticket details on every email, so each button knows exactly which ticket it belongs to. `send-3`

## Step 4: The click

1. Here's what your customer receives: a friendly message, and one clear button. `click-1`
2. When they click, Klick-Zen applies the actions you chose to their ticket, through its secure connection to your Zendesk. `click-2`
3. It's a normal ticket update, so your triggers and automations react just as they would to an agent's change. `click-3`

## After the click

1. Your customer lands on a confirmation page with your logo, colors and message, hosted by Klick-Zen. `after-1`
2. And back in Zendesk, the ticket is already solved, with nothing left for your agents to do. `after-2`

## Step 5: Safe by design

1. Is it safe? Yes. Each link works only for its own ticket, and a click can only do what you set up. `safe-1`
2. Customers never need to log in, and automated email scanners can't set a button off by accident. `safe-2`

## Next steps

1. Faster resolutions, cleaner ticket data, and a customer experience that feels effortless. `outro-1`
2. Find Klick-Zen on the Zendesk Marketplace, and give your customers a one-click way to get things done. `outro-2`
