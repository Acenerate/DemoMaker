# Klick-Zen Online Template Generator

An animated walkthrough, about two minutes

21 lines, 331 words (about 132 seconds of narration at 150 words per minute).

## Introduction

1. Writing a good customer email, then building the buttons to go in it, takes time. The Klick-Zen Online Template Generator does both for you, for free. `intro-1`
2. Pick a few options, and it writes a ready-to-paste Zendesk email with Klick-Zen buttons already laid out. `intro-2`

## Step 1: Build the email

1. Open the generator and start on the left. When does the email go out? We'll choose Solved. `build-1`
2. What should the customer do? Confirm it's fixed. Then pick a tone: Friendly or Professional. `build-2`
3. The preview on the right updates as you go: a subject, a message, and buttons that fit. Show sample data fills in example values. `build-3`
4. Prefer a head start? Pick one of the ready-made templates instead, for approvals, refunds, escalations and more. `build-4`
5. Under Making it a working email, open What each button does, to see exactly what every button will do to the ticket. `build-5`

## Step 2: Download the buttons

1. The buttons start out as placeholders. To make them work, click Download buttons. `download-1`
2. You get one button file, with each button's text, colors and actions already set. `download-2`

## Step 3: Import them into Klick-Zen

1. In Klick-Zen, under Available KlickButtons, click Import buttons, and choose that file. `import-1`
2. Each button is listed with a short summary of what it does. Click Import. `import-2`
3. Klick-Zen creates them all in one step, and shows each button's HTML, ready to copy. `import-3`

## Step 4: Link them in the generator

1. Back in the generator, paste each button's HTML into the box next to it. `link-1`
2. The preview switches to your real Klick-Zen buttons, and the note confirms: All buttons are linked. `link-2`

## Step 5: Add it to Zendesk

1. Now click Copy HTML. This email goes out when a ticket is solved, so it belongs in a trigger. `zendesk-1`
2. Paste it into the trigger's Email body, use the generator's subject, and save. `zendesk-2`

## What your customer sees

1. Your customer gets a clear, friendly email, with two simple choices. `result-1`
2. One click on Yes, all set, and they land on a thank-you page. Nothing to type. `result-2`
3. Back in Zendesk, the answer is already on the ticket, with no reply to read. `result-3`

## Next steps

1. A finished email and working buttons, in minutes. `outro-1`
2. Try the free Online Template Generator, and find Klick-Zen on the Zendesk Marketplace. `outro-2`
