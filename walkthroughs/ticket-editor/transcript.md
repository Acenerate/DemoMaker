# Using Buttons in the Ticket Editor

An animated walkthrough, about one minute

11 lines, 169 words (about 68 seconds of narration at 150 words per minute).

## Introduction

1. Sometimes the best place for a one-click button is right in the middle of a reply. `intro-1`
2. With Klick-Zen, your agents can drop one into any ticket response, with just a click. `intro-2`

## Step 1: Insert a button

1. Write your reply in the Zendesk ticket editor as usual, and place your cursor where the button should go. `insert-1`
2. Then click the Klick-Zen icon in the editor toolbar. `insert-2`
3. A pop-up shows the buttons in your account, each one pictured just as it looks, so you pick by sight. `insert-3`
4. Click one, and it lands right in your message, exactly as your customer will see it. `insert-4`

## Step 2: Offer a choice

1. Want to give your customer a choice? Add a second button right beside the first. `choice-1`
2. Klick-Zen fills in this ticket's details as each button goes in, so every link is ready to go. `choice-2`
3. Add a closing line, send your reply, and you're done. `choice-3`
