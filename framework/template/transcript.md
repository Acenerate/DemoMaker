# Walkthrough template

Copy this folder to start a new walkthrough

7 lines, 67 words (about 27 seconds of narration at 150 words per minute).

## Introduction

1. This line is shown while the title card fades in. `intro-1`
2. Each line is one caption. The next one starts when this line and its animation have both finished. `intro-2`

## Step 1: A custom scene

1. Move the cursor, click, and type into your own markup. `custom-1`
2. Then show the result. `custom-2`

## Step 2: Kit screens

1. Kit screens are ready-made. Zoom in, pick from dropdowns, and update the model. `kit-1`
2. Highlight what matters with a ring. `kit-2`

## Wrap up

1. Finish on a clear next step. `outro-1`
